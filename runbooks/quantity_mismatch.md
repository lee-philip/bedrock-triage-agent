# Quantity Mismatch

Trade quantity differs between buyer and seller records.

## Symptoms

- Delivery/acceptance shows different quantity than trade ticket
- Invoicing discrepancy
- Reconciliation fails
- Counterparty disputes quantity received

## Likely Root Causes

1. Manual entry error
2. Delivery split across multiple shipments not captured
3. Shrinkage or spoilage (commodities)
4. Different measurement units

## Checks

- Compare trade ticket quantity vs. delivery documents
- Verify measurement units match
- Check delivery history for partial deliveries
- Review weighbridge or measurement records

## Fix Steps

1. Identify trade and quantity discrepancy
2. Obtain delivery documents and measurement records
3. Determine actual quantity delivered
4. If delivery issue, coordinate with Logistics
5. If measurement issue, recalibrate and re-measure
6. Adjust trade settlement accordingly

## Escalation

If counterparty disputes records, escalate to Dispute Resolution. If systematic measurement issues, engage Quality Assurance.
