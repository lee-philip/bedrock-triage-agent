# Insufficient Cash

Counterparty does not have sufficient funds to settle the trade.

## Symptoms

- Trade returns "insufficient funds" status
- Settlement delay while funds are arranged
- Counterparty requests settlement extension
- Overdraft fees incurred

## Likely Root Causes

1. Counterparty liquidity issue
2. Timing mismatch in fund transfer
3. Incorrect trade value calculation
4. Counterparty credit deterioration

## Checks

- Verify trade value and currency
- Check counterparty credit status
- Review funding transfer schedules
- Verify settlement currency account is correct

## Fix Steps

1. Identify trade and counterparty
2. Contact counterparty to confirm funding status
3. If timing issue, arrange settlement extension
4. If liquidity issue, propose alternative settlement arrangement
5. Update counterparty credit risk assessment

## Escalation

If counterparty cannot settle within 48 hours, escalate to Credit Risk team. If counterparty disputes trade value, engage Legal.
